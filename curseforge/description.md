# DrinkZis

**A floating potion bag.** One round button opens a compact panel with every potion in your bags, sorted by category — click to drink.

Made for **WoW Forever**.

## Features

- **Sorted by category**
  - **Health**: healing potions, healthstones.
  - **Mana**: mana potions, mana gems.
  - **Combat**: other potions (swiftness, free action, protection, rage…) and energy items (Thistle Tea).
  - **Buffs**: elixirs, flasks, scrolls.
  - **Debuffs**: anti-venom and potions that cure poison, disease or curse.
- One slot per item, with total count and cooldown.
- **Opens in combat**: the round button works in combat too, and the bag can open automatically when combat starts and close when it ends.
- **Themes**: Dark, Gold, Arcane, Fel, Blood, WoWtesk (classic WoW UI look).
- **Fade out of combat** (option): the round button turns transparent outside combat, and opaque again on hover or in combat.
- **Languages**: English and French interface (Auto follows the game client). Potions are recognised on English and French clients whatever the interface language.
- Choose which categories are shown.

## Usage

- Click the round button to open / close the bag.
- **Shift + drag** the round button to move everything.
- **Minimap icon**: left-click opens / closes the bag (works in combat), right-click opens the options, drag to move it around the minimap. `/dz minimap` shows / hides it.
- `/dz` opens the options (also in *Esc → Options → AddOns*).

Note: the game forbids creating buttons during combat, so a potion looted mid-fight shows up when combat ends. Counts and cooldowns update live.

# DrinkZis (FR)

**Un sac à potions flottant.** Une icône ronde ouvre un cadre avec toutes les potions de vos sacs, rangées par catégorie : vie, mana, combat, buffs, debuffs.

- Un emplacement par objet, avec quantité totale et recharge.
- Ouverture automatique en combat, fermeture à la sortie (option).
- Thèmes : Sombre, Doré, Arcane, Gangrène, Sang, WoWtesk (look de l’interface WoW).
- Interface en anglais ou en français (Auto = langue du jeu).
- Option : icône ronde transparente hors combat.
- Icône autour de la minimap : clic gauche = sac, clic droit = options.
- `/dz` : options. **Maj + glisser** l'icône : déplacer.
